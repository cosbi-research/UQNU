Following the model import and assessment stage, the identifiability and uncertainty of the fixed-effect model parameters estimated from the data will be evaluated. Given the limited number of subjects per dose, inter-individual variability will not be modeled and estimation will be restricted to fixed effects, while parameters fixed to literature values will be treated as known constants and excluded from the uncertainty analysis. In addition, parameters of the PK, biodistribution, and PD models will be examined and discussed with respect to whether they should be considered product-specific, receptor-specific, or target-specific, in order to inform subsequent model generalization across therapeutics.
